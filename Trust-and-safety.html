THE USEONCE PROMISE



Borrow Better. Lend With Confidence.



USEONCE brings people together through the things they own and the things they need. We believe sharing starts with honesty, grows through trust, and thrives when everyone treats each other’s belongings with care.

OUR SHARED VALUES

Good things are better when they're shared.

USEONCE gives useful belongings more opportunities to be enjoyed. Instead of buying things we only need occasionally, we can share what we have, reduce waste and make everyday experiences more accessible. Every exchange begins with honesty, respect and a little consideration.

THE ESSENTIALS

A little care goes a long way.

01 Be honest. Describe items accurately and agree on costs and conditions.

02 Be responsible. Care for borrowed items and return them as agreed.

03 Communicate clearly. Keep arrangements clear and address problems respectfully.

FIX IT

IF THINGS GO WRONG

Let's find a fair way forward. For late returns, damage or misunderstandings, communicate calmly, keep a record of the issue and work towards a reasonable resolution.
